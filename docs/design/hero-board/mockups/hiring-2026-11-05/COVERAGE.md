## What the checks cover

"Overflow" is the document's width beyond the viewport. "Clipped" names elements whose content is wider than their hidden-overflow box (visually-hidden elements excluded). "Hit targets" measures the first step, small action and filter chip: the visible box and the box its absolutely placed ::before extends it to (`proposed.css` §7 for the steps and this family's actions; the app's own rule for filter chips). The map row counts the tiles loaded in the box and whether the pin, zoom buttons and attribution are present. The viewport column is the first screen as it opens, with the dock where it really sits; in the full-height exports the dock is drawn at the page's end so it covers nothing. A squeezed column or an occluded field is checked by eye on those captures, not by the manifest.
